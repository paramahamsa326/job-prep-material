# LinkedIn Outreach Messages — Under 300 Characters

Each message is written for a LinkedIn connection request and stays within 300 characters, including spaces and punctuation.

## 1. Warm and professional

Hi Sruthi! Hope you're doing well. I'm exploring opportunities at SeatGeek and would love to connect. I'd enjoy hearing about your experience, the company culture, and any advice you have for someone interested in joining the team. Thanks, Param

## 2. Friendly and conversational

Hi Sruthi! I came across your profile while checking out SeatGeek. I'd love to connect and hear what it's like working there—from the culture and people to any exciting job opportunities. Looking forward to connecting! —Param

## 3. Short and direct

Hi Sruthi! I'm interested in opportunities at SeatGeek and would love to connect. I'd be happy to hear about your experience, the company culture, and any tips for someone hoping to join the team. Thanks! —Param

## 4. Relationship focused

Hi Sruthi! I'm always glad to meet others in the field and came across your profile while learning about SeatGeek. I'd love to connect, hear about the culture, and learn more about opportunities with the company. Best, Param

## 5. Advice focused

Hi Sruthi! I'm exploring roles at SeatGeek and would really value your perspective. I'd love to connect and hear about the culture, your experience, and any advice you might have for someone interested in joining. Thanks, Param

## 6. Casual coffee-chat style

Hi Sruthi! SeatGeek caught my interest, and I'd love to learn what working there is really like. Would be great to connect and chat about the people, culture, and potential job opportunities whenever you have a moment. Cheers, Param

## 7. Role specific

Hi Sruthi! I'm interested in the [Job Title] role at SeatGeek and would love to connect. I'd enjoy learning about your experience, the company culture, and what the team looks for in new hires. Thanks! —Param

## 8. Shared interest

Hi Sruthi! It's great to find someone else in [shared field]. I'm exploring opportunities at SeatGeek and would love to connect, hear about your experience and the culture, and learn what it's like to be part of the team. —Param

## 9. Curious and enthusiastic

Hi Sruthi! I've been learning about SeatGeek and am excited by the possibility of joining the company. I'd love to connect and get your take on the culture, your experience, and any opportunities worth exploring. Thanks, Param

## 10. Follow-up after connecting

Hi Sruthi! Thanks for connecting. I'm exploring opportunities at SeatGeek and would love to hear about your experience, the company culture, and any advice you have for someone hoping to join the team. I appreciate it! —Param

## Reusable placeholders

Replace **Sruthi**, **SeatGeek**, **[Job Title]**, or **[shared field]** as needed. Recheck the character count when inserting longer names or titles.
