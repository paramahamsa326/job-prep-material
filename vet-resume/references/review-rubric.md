# Resume Review Rubric

## Targeted dimensions

Judge each as **strong**, **adequate**, **weak**, or **not demonstrated**, citing resume evidence.

| Dimension | Examine |
|---|---|
| Minimum qualifications | Years, education, certifications, required domain and tool experience |
| Relevant outcomes | Results comparable to the role's central outcomes |
| Scope and seniority | Ownership, ambiguity, scale, influence, leadership, architecture, or strategy |
| Depth | Applied skills in context, recency, complexity, and production use |
| Impact | Verified business, customer, reliability, quality, cost, speed, revenue, or risk outcomes |
| Discoverability | Important truthful terminology visible in context, not listed without evidence |
| Credibility | Consistent chronology, plausible claims, clear ownership, and restrained language |

Use this evidence table:

| Priority | Job requirement | Resume evidence | Coverage | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Must | ... | ... | Strong / Partial / Absent / Unclear | Keep / Clarify / Add verified evidence / Genuine gap |

Treat **required**, **minimum**, and central responsibilities as must-have; **preferred**, **bonus**, and **nice to have** as preferred; and label anything inferred from context.

## Generic dimensions

| Dimension | Questions |
|---|---|
| Positioning | Is target level and professional identity clear in the top third? |
| Progression | Do increasing scope, complexity, leadership, or specialization emerge? |
| Achievements | Do bullets show outcomes and contribution rather than duties? |
| Evidence density | Are claims supported by scope, constraints, methods, stakeholders, or verified metrics? |
| Relevance | Is space weighted toward recent, distinctive, and marketable experience? |
| Readability | Can a recruiter scan titles, employers, dates, skills, and major wins quickly? |
| Consistency | Are tense, punctuation, dates, capitalization, and terminology consistent? |
| Credibility | Are claims precise, defensible, and proportionate to the evidence? |

## Bullet diagnostic

Test whether a bullet explains what changed, what the candidate owned, how it was done when differentiating, the scale or complexity, and the verified outcome. Not every bullet needs every element.

Use metrics only when provided. Without one, prefer a truthful qualitative result over an invented number.

## Priorities and output

- **Critical:** eligibility, credibility, or comprehension problem.
- **High:** materially affects fit or recruiter understanding.
- **Medium:** strengthens evidence, clarity, or differentiation.
- **Low:** polish with limited decision impact.

For targeted reviews, deliver verdict, requirement table, top changes, evidence questions, and selected rewrites. For generic reviews, deliver current narrative, supported role families, strongest evidence, top changes, and rewrites.

Avoid false precision. If scoring helps, use a transparent scale and show supporting evidence; never call it an ATS score.
